**Build, in this PR:**

- **The `act` link method, home → node.** The home asks a node `act {rpc, params, caller}` and the node runs that RPC on its own host — `send`, `keys`, `kill`, `close`, `mode`, `remove`, `decide`, `set_grants`, `set_controllers`, the wrap-up, and `create` — through its own handler with **no gate of its own**: the home gated it, and the node is the executor. The node's result, or its refusal in words, is the home's reply to the caller. A node that is asked to act on a record whose `host` is not its own refuses (*not my host*); a home routes an act only to the node whose name is the record's `host`. While that host's link is down the act is refused as *runs on <host>: unreachable since <since> — <why>* — never queued (§4.4a "refused, not queued").
- **The gate reads one graph.** `_gate` and the mail gates take the union of the home's own records and every `self.remote[*]` map, keyed by address (`id@host` for a remote record, bare for the home's), so a lead on kmaster holding `control` over a member on a node passes the same two-part check it passes today, and a session on a node that acts on a kmaster session through the home is gated identically. Mail across hosts stays refused by name (**step 5**); only the *acting* RPCs cross in this step. `self.sessions` itself is never widened: the tick, the anchor rule and the pane reads stay this host's.
- **`create` with a `host`.** `rpc_create` takes `host` (default: the home's own); for another host it is an `act` to that node, whose `create` runs the anchor rule and occupancy over *its* records and its checkout; the home then adopts the record from the node's next report, and the caller's reply carries `id@host`. The home's own occupancy check does **not** reach across hosts in this step — two machines' `/home/x/repo` are two directories; the one case where they are one directory is a container node on the home's machine, which is **3c.4**, not yours.
- **A team lands on another host.** `host:` on a team definition in `org.yml` (and a repo's `.agentorc.yml`), read by `teams.plan` into each member's create; `ao team start` keeps its all-or-nothing check, which for a member on another host asks that node whether the checkout exists (a small `stat`-shaped link method, or fold it into a dry-run `create` — decide and say so) and refuses the whole team while any member's host is unreachable (§4.4a "Teams across hosts"); `teamrun.stop_members` degrades **per member** — an unreachable member is reported, the rest are still wrapped up — rather than aborting on the first refusal (the 3b leftover).
- **The two small leftovers**: `/term/<id@host>` answers an explicit *runs on <host>: the terminal across the link is not built (TD-057 3c.5 / step 4b)* rather than relying on tmux's miss; and the *acts across the link are not built* refusal in `_dispatch` goes away, replaced by the routing above.
