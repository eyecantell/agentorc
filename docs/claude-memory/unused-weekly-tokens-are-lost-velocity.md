The account has a fixed amount of usage per week. Whatever is unused when the weekly window resets is gone, so an idle team before the reset is lost velocity, not saved money. Paul, 2026-10-06, correcting the anchor: *"once the reset hits any unused tokens are lost — it's a matter of velocity — … the loss is time."* He had set the usage gate's reserve to zero for the last two hours of the week.

**Why:** the anchor had advised deferring work because "the tokens aren't really lost", and that was wrong. Paul plans the week around spending the whole window.

**How to apply:** when the week's window is near its reset with room left (`ao gate`), look for idle sessions and why they are idle: the package split, an unanswered ask, a stalled restart. Hand them pickable work, and run reviews or live checks in parallel. Don't defer work to "save" the budget. The 5-hour window is the real limit within a week.
