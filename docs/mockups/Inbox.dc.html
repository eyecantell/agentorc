Design notes, not page text. The rail (TD-129, Paul's shape, 2026-09-24): under the title, which has the top line to itself; left of the column, sticky, three groups of toggles — the sections in the page's order, the teams with their Needs you counts, the coarse kinds — and the find box. Nothing pressed here, so every count is the whole. Within a group picks are OR'd, across groups AND'd, the find a fourth group; nothing picked means all. The first group is Urgency — what orders the page — not Sections, which names nothing a person looks for, and not State, a session's word and a kind below. The typed team: box is gone: a filter that is a control is not typed. One centred column (1100 px at most) beside it — a queue reads in order, top to bottom. A section is a heading, not a box: its name, its count, and an i mark that holds the blurb (drawn open on Needs you). A row is a card: its own surface, a hover state (first card) and a keyboard focus ring (second) — j / k move the ring, Enter opens a mail row's page, o, a, d, r, s, x press the row's own Open, Allow, Deny, Reply, Snooze and Dismiss or Done (§4.5a keys, TD-124). The state pill and the kind label are flat and unbordered so they never read as buttons; everything bordered is a control. Suggested answers stay in their own dashed group, in quotation marks.
