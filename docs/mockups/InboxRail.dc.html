Steering, Waiting on them, Answered for you and FYI are not picked — press them in the rail, or Clear filters.
Design notes, not page text. The rail with picks (TD-129): ao-grind and Needs you pressed — Paul's workflow, one team's Needs you worked through, then the next team's. Every count is a count of rows on the page now: a picked line reads its share, an unpicked line in a group with a pick reads 0 of all, dimmed (it contributes nothing until pressed, and all says what it would bring), an unpicked line in a group without a pick reads its share under the other groups' picks. The title row is Inbox alone: the picks are on the left, so a showing … line and a needs-you pill beside it were noise. Needs you reads 2 of 6 because the top bar's number is never filtered. A section not picked is not drawn; one picked and emptied by the other groups would draw its heading and its empty line — a filter shows or hides rows and never re-orders the queue. Every count reads n of all while anything is picked or typed and a plain number otherwise. Clear filters appears at the rail's head while anything is picked or typed and clears the lot. The URL is /inbox?team=ao-grind&sec=needs: a filtered Inbox is a link, remembered per browser for a bare /inbox. Not designed: a preview pane (TD-129 option b) — at any width a row's text opens its page (Inbox — message).
