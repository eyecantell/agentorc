Org page: declutter round

The live Org page re-captured read-only on the evening of 2026-10-08 for round 4, so the numbers are that moment's. Both shots show the same data: the "after" applies the proposed changes to that captured page, so only the design differs.

After: one header row per team, aligned bars, own-lane counts on the state pills, WAITING, the kind bar by who an entry waits on.

What changes

mine and show command runs become filter words

Both controls leave the title row. The filter box takes mine and kind:command next to team: and state:, and its placeholder lists them.

The rollup's bars line up

Every facet header reserves the height of the day/week/month picker, and the phase bar and the opened/closed blocks are drawn at one height (26px). The repo facet's bars get the same height.

The Agents text moves into an i

"in urgency order, as the cards sort · a pill filters the page to that state" becomes the tooltip on an i beside AGENTS. Needs you also drops its "0 answer needed" row at zero, as asked you already does.

Shell and + New session merge into + New ▾

One button with two choices, Session and Shell. A team's own sessions still start from its + card.

The team header fits on one row

The header keeps the name, host / repo, a flow: td chip (its stages in the tooltip, linked to Settings), the marks, Wind down, Stop now, the fold and the i. Dropped from the header: the second "9 sessions", the flow strip, and the flow dropdown (Settings already has the same picker per team). Members…, "defined in … changed by PR", Open file and not concluded: move into the i panel under a Definition line. flow changed · Apply stays on the header because it asks you to act.

✉ n shows folded or not

The team's unread total sits beside the flow chip in both states. TD-071 drew it only while folded; that rule is reversed.

The lanes line leaves the repo facet

Its full text becomes an i on TECHNICAL DEBT. Each card counts the entries its own lane takes that nobody holds, attached to its state pill in the bar's colour for that kind. When several sessions share one lane the count reads n/k: two grinders on the shared grinder lane show 14/2 on both cards. The same goes for two designers. Soft while the member works or waits; solid when it has said it is out of work with entries in its lane. No count at 0.

WORKING 14 its own lane, alone WORKING 14/2 a lane shared by 2 grinders WAITING 9 designer waiting, 9 to design IDLE 9 out of work, 9 waiting: look

In the shot only the anchor seat carries a count (WORKING 14): its lane takes 14 unheld entries. The grinders' shared lane holds TD-422 alone, which grinder-ao-2 is working, so neither grinder card has a count. The first capture's "18 pickable" was the team's sum, mostly the anchor's lane: its builds and the live checks whose builds are live.

The kind bar counts by who an entry waits on

pickable (a build a lane can take) · design (the designer can take it) · for you · live check · blocked (waits on another open entry) · evaluation (the anchor's) · other. The kinds are tested in this order: for you, then blocked (whatever its Kind), design, live check, pickable, evaluation. Tonight's reading: 5 · 0 · 8 · 12 · 15 · 13 · 0 of 53. Other should be empty; an entry that lands there is flagged by the ledger reader.

Today's bar hides most of this. Its 16 "pickable" were 5 builds plus 11 live checks whose builds are live; its 10 "design-first" were designed entries waiting on their builds; its grey 19 "other" were 13 anchor evaluations, 3 blocked builds, 2 live checks not yet live and 1 anchor decision (TD-077). Under the new order that decision counts as blocked: TD-077 waits on TD-075, and its decision Kind is stale (decided 2026-09-19). Why the 13 live checks exist is TD-426.

A WAITING state

An idle session reads WAITING when it holds a claim whose PR is open (waiting · review #1302, docs-audit-ao-1 in the shot), or has an open ask to you about a TD (waiting · you). A session in ao wait is not WAITING: the wait holds its turn open, so it reads WORKING. It has its own colour, its own place in the sort, and its own Agents pill. IDLE then means it holds nothing. A session never waits on a build; an entry can, which is what the bar's blocked says.

"techlead", never "reader"

The page names the seat you know: waiting · review #1302 with the techlead, PRs waiting for the techlead, Settings' the techlead's queue. Where a flow makes someone else the reader, the page names that seat's role.

Doing ages read 0m

"just now" becomes 0m, so the age column is only as wide as its widest age.

The + card reads New session

"your session in ao-grind" becomes "New session". The team card is the context.

TECHNICAL DEBT (56 OPEN) stays on one line

Section labels never wrap. Where the header runs out of room, the window picker drops to its own line on the right, not the label.

Doing loses "the team's ao doing calls"

The label and the rows say it.

The rollup drops what a lone team already says

While one team is live, the rollup keeps Agents and Needs you; its TDs-in-motion and PR facets come back with a second live team.

A team with nothing live draws no summary

dc-grind's "no repo here" and "nothing claimed" boxes go; its header and Start stay.

The summary's columns follow their content

Repo and Doing get more width, TDs in motion less (1.15 : 0.85 : 1.2). The cost: TD titles in that facet truncate sooner; the reference and holder stay whole.
