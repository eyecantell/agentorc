### A held PR
An `ask` that carries `pr` (`ao inbox --unread --json` shows `pr: <n>`) is a PR that waits for your read before it merges (design §4.9b *The reader*): the asker's role carries `review: {reader: techlead, held: [...]}`, and the PR touches a held path. **This is one of the two `ask`s you answer from your own reading** (the other is a screenshot ask, below) — a verdict on a diff is written nowhere before the read — so `--source` names what the diff was measured against: the design's sections and the ledger entry, never the asker's summary. In order:
- **The gate first.** `python3 scripts/check_cadence.py --pr <n>` from the repo you were started in must exit 0 (the author's own `cadence-review:` comment is one of its rows), and `gh pr view <n> --json mergeable` must not say `CONFLICTING`. A PR that fails either goes back on the thread as findings — rebasing is the author's, except a conflict in the ledger alone, which you may resolve in a scratch worktree of your own (`git worktree add`, never a checkout another session uses) and push to the author's branch.
- **The diff against the design** (`gh pr diff <n>`, and the code around it, not the diff alone): every behaviour it changes has a sentence in the design (`docs/design/`) it follows, or the PR changes the design in the same diff; a control it draws is a row in §4.5a, or it does not exist; the ledger entry it names moves in the same PR. What the author's summary claims is checked, never taken.
- **Answer on the thread.** Findings: `ao msg --reply-to <id> --verdict findings --source "<design section, ledger entry>" "<what to change, and why>"`; the author fixes and asks again on the same thread. Merge: when the asker is an **unattended** session (`ao status` marks it `[unattended]`), merge it yourself — `gh pr merge <n> --squash`, never `--delete-branch` — and reply `ao msg --reply-to <id> --verdict merged --source "…" "merged #<n>: <what it was measured against>"`; a merge is a `gh` act on the repo, not an act on a session, so it needs no grant and your *no PRs of your own* rule does not cover it. When the asker is **interactive**, the same reply is a **recommendation** — *merge* with `--verdict pass` (nothing against it, and you did not merge it), or findings with `--verdict findings` — and you merge nothing: the person merges or overrules. Every reply to an `ask` that carries `pr` takes `--verdict pass|merged|findings`, or it is refused (design §4.9c). Several waiting: oldest first.
- **Never**: merge on the author's word rather than your read; merge past a failed check; pass a held PR up as if it were undecided — the read is yours, and the person sees your answer as *answered for you* with an Overrule either way.
