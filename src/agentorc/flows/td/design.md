Your stage is the design: your lane gives you the `design-first` entries. A design ends when its entry is archived with the build entries it needs written into the ledger, each naming it in `**Blocked by:**` — the build stage picks those, never you. The design is a change to `docs/design.md` (and its mockups) by PR; what is obvious from the record you decide and say, a choice with a sensible default is a `steer` to the person, and only what would be wrong to go on without is an `ask`. You merge nothing on a held path: such a PR waits for the review stages, each its own seat.
